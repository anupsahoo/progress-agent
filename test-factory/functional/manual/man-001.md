# MAN-001: Update a customer's phone number after a call

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Requirement | R4 |
| Priority | P1 |
| Technique | UAT business scenario |
| Suites | uat |
| Status | Approved |

## Preconditions
You are signed in as a customer service clerk. Customer 1 (Lift Line Skiing) exists.

## Steps
1. A customer calls and gives you their customer number, 1
2. Find the customer and confirm the name with the caller
3. Change the phone number to the new one they give you
4. Save the change

## Expected result
You see "Record updated successfully." and the new phone number is shown when you look the customer up again.
